Fitness Knowledge

Workout Tips

Expert advice to maximize your training, nutrition, and recovery.

💧
Hydrate Properly
Drink at least 500ml water 30 minutes before training. During intense workouts, aim for 200ml every 15 minutes. Dehydration reduces performance by up to 30%.
😴
Sleep is Training
Muscle is built during sleep, not in the gym. Aim for 7-9 hours per night. Poor sleep increases cortisol, breaking down muscle you've worked hard to build.
🥩
Protein Timing
Consume 20-40g of protein within 30 minutes post-workout. This anabolic window is crucial for muscle repair and growth. Chicken, eggs, or whey protein are ideal.
📈
Progressive Overload
Gradually increase weight, reps, or intensity each week. Your muscles only grow when challenged beyond their current capacity. Track your lifts religiously.
🧘
Warm Up Properly
Spend 10 minutes on dynamic stretching before lifting. Cold muscles tear more easily. A proper warm-up improves performance and dramatically reduces injury risk.
🗓️
Rest Day Strategy
Take 1-2 rest days per week. Use them for light walking, stretching, or yoga. Overtraining leads to plateaus and injuries. Recovery is part of the program.
